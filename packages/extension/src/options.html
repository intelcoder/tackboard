To use pages opened from your computer (file://), open this extension's card in chrome://extensions and turn on "Allow access to file URLs".

    Screenshots

    Lets a comment carry a picture of the element it is about. Chrome asks for access to all sites because it will not take a picture of a tab without it. A picture is taken only when you tick "Attach screenshot" on a comment, and it goes to your own daemon, nowhere else.

    Add a site

    Pages on localhost and files on your computer work by themselves while the daemon is running, with nothing to add. Adding a site puts the comment bar on every page of that site. The daemon token stays inside this extension. Add only sites you control, never one that real visitors use.
