Choose which pages get the comment bar. Comments go through the daemon on your computer to the coding agent working in that project.

Sites

    Pages on localhost and files on your computer pair by themselves while the daemon runs, and appear here once they have. There is nothing to add for those.

    Files opened from your computer (file://) also need a switch only you can flip: open this extension's card in chrome://extensions and turn on "Allow access to file URLs".

    Other sites

    A staging host or a tunnel address, for instance. The comment bar goes on every page of that site and the daemon token stays inside this extension. Add only sites you control, never one that real visitors use.

    Screenshots

    Lets a comment carry a picture of the element it is about. Chrome asks for access to all sites because it takes no picture of a tab without that. A picture is taken only when you tick "Attach screenshot" on a comment, and it goes to your own daemon, nowhere else.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Alt+Shift+K
    Add a comment
    Alt+Shift+X
    Hide or show the overlay
    Alt+Shift+L
    Open the comments panel

    They do nothing while you type in a text field.
